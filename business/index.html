Your business runs on a dozen tools that don't talk

Orders live in one app, invoices in another, customers in a third. Every report is a spreadsheet someone rebuilds by hand. And the AI tools on offer want your data in their cloud, on their terms.

  1. Your SaaS tools Storefront, accounting, CRM, payments, email, scheduling
  2. Your warehouse One clean copy of your data, in an account you own
  3. Your agents Draft, check, and flag work using that data
  4. You decide People approve anything that matters

What we build

Three pieces, each useful on its own, designed to work together.

  1. 01 A data warehouse you own Your business data in one place, in your own cloud account, stored in open formats such as Postgres. Clean history you can query, report on, and keep if you ever leave us.
  2. 02 Integrations across your SaaS Connectors that pull from the tools you already use, then clean and match records, so the same customer, product, or job lines up across every system.
  3. 03 AI agents for your industry Agents that draft replies, documents, and quotes, answer questions about your numbers, and flag problems early. Each one can only use the tools and data you allow, and every action is logged.

Agents shaped to your industry

Examples of agents we can build. Each one drafts or flags; a person makes the call.

  • Ecommerce and retail
    • Reorder alerts from sales velocity and stock levels
    • Order exception triage across storefront, payments, and shipping
    • Customer support replies drafted from order history
    • Margin by product and channel, refreshed daily
  • Professional services
    • Client intake that gathers documents and checks for conflicts
    • First drafts of letters, proposals, and reports from your templates
    • Time and billing reconciliation before invoices go out
    • Pipeline and utilization reporting across CRM and accounting
  • Field and local services
    • Quote drafts from job notes and past pricing
    • Scheduling suggestions that account for crews and travel
    • Review responses drafted for approval
    • Job costing that ties hours and materials to invoices
  • Regulated and compliance-heavy
    • Document and label checks against your rules, flagged for review
    • Privacy and data requests tracked to completion
    • Audit trails that show what was checked, by whom, and when
    • Policy questions answered from your own procedures

Built so you are never stuck

You own it
The warehouse, code, and credentials live in accounts you control. We work in them; we don't hold them.
You can leave
Open formats and written documentation mean another team, or your own, can take over without a rebuild.
You can see what agents did
Every agent action is recorded: what it read, what it proposed, and who approved it.
You choose the AI
Agents aren't tied to one model provider. You decide which models are used and what data they can see.

How an engagement works

  1. 01 Map your stack A conversation about the tools you use, the numbers you need, and the work that eats your week.
  2. 02 Build the foundation Set up the warehouse in your account and connect your most important tools first.
  3. 03 Add agents Start with one workflow, measure whether it helps, then extend to the next.
  4. 04 Hand over You get the code, the documentation, and the access. Keep us on for support, or don't.
