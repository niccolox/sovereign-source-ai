# sovsrc.llm landing page (llm/index.html)

Scope: the landing page for the sovsrc.llm project, generated by `_tools/build_llm_updates.py`. Visitor mode: Persuade. The visual world is the existing site; this is a new surface inside it, not a new world. The update stream moved to `llm/updates.html`.

Audience: creators, rights specialists, and model builders who assess the idea. Job: understand what sovsrc.llm proposes and decide whether to read the specification or start a conversation. Action: email hello@sovsrc.ai; the quiet action is the GitHub repository. Proof: only draft documents exist. Constraints: describe every plan, rate, and term as a proposal; no invented product, model, payout, or partner; no excluded-market language.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page owns one idea: the proposed creator share of revenue, drawn to scale. It refuses the category-default hero plus three feature cards plus pricing table.

OWN-WORLD: Black ground, white display type, gray body, hairline rules, ghost text behind. Components are rule-lined rows, the dotted flow rule, and the Band Black guarantees band. No cards, no color, no red.

STORY: The visitor reads that an LLM can ask permission, give credit, and pay by agreement. They see how large the proposed pool is against all revenue, then how the pool divides. They learn that only drafts exist, and they read the specification or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline top left at display scale, a two-sentence description with the specification-stage status beneath, the email button and a quiet link to the repository under it. The permission flow rule starts at the fold edge.

FORM: "The pool, to scale", code-led, composed directly. No concept roll ran and there is no seed key; the user asked for the page in one sentence and did not waive the roll, so this is a recorded deviation. Signature: one 1px rule is all eligible revenue; its first 20% is white; two hairlines open that segment into a second full-width rule divided 60/20/20, each track labeled under its own length. Motion grammar: on first view the second rule draws out once from the 20% width, from an already-visible state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
