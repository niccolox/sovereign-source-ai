Production proposal adds a licensed research assistant and model approval conditions

The new production proposal prioritizes a research assistant for a bounded, licensed technical collection, with source evidence and agreed contributor payments. The project remains at specification stage.

12 Oct 2026 · Committed, not on GitHub yet

Proposed first service

The proposal puts source permissions, attribution receipts and creator accounting before model adaptation. It separates citations, contractual payment rights and completed payments.

Model approval conditions

The proposed design retains the US-only and no-Chinese-model-lineage requirements. It keeps model routes disabled when required approval conditions fail or remain unresolved. It excludes production conversations and frontier responses from training unless specific reuse permissions exist.
