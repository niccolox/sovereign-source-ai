New Super Anthropocene article examines proposed contributor rewards

Proposed contributor rewards

The article proposes licence payments, commissioned expertise, maintenance fees and revenue shares for defined uses. Proposed agreements would specify eligible contributions, payment responsibilities and reviewable calculations.

Recognition and permission

The article distinguishes permission, attribution and compensation. It presents Super Intellectual Property as a proposed recognition concept, not statutory ownership. It calls for preserving public-domain knowledge, open licences and independent discovery.

The article treats the business case for proposed contributor payments as a hypothesis that needs testing. It identifies administration costs, low-quality submissions and disputes as risks. It also calls for worker participation, affordable access and attention to environmental costs.
