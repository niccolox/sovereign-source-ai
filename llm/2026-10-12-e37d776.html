SkyRL assessment adds a proposed post-training option

The new assessment proposes SkyRL as an optional, self-hosted post-training framework for the research assistant. The work remains research and a proposed pilot, with no installation, GPU training or deployment performed.

Proposed training option

The assessment proposes keeping Nemotron 3 Ultra and the NVIDIA training path as the reference plan. SkyRL compatibility with Ultra remains unverified.

Compatibility checks

The proposal requires reproducible checks before adopting SkyRL for Ultra. These cover checkpoint loading, numerical agreement, optimization, weight transfer, save and resume, export, memory and throughput.
