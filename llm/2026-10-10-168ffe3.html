Research assistant proposal

Proposed contributor payments

The draft proposes allocating 20% of eligible collected revenue to contributors. Proposed pool shares allocate 60% to training and licensed corpus, 20% to prompt and workflow reuse, and 20% to adopted ideas and suggestions. These proposed contractual terms do not automatically reward every submission or ordinary prompt.

Permission and attribution proposals

Proposed admission rules separate permissions for training, retrieval, quotation and other uses. The proposal requires explicit consent for shared training on customer conversations. The documents distinguish supporting citations from proof of ownership or training influence.
