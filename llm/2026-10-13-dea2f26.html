Research adds ten commercial options for training an LLM from scratch

New procurement research compares ten commercial training offerings and proposes AWS SageMaker AI as the general-purpose managed-training candidate. The shortlist reflects proposed project fit, not measured performance.

13 Oct 2026 · commit dea2f26

Proposed training candidates

The research proposes comparing AWS SageMaker AI with Together GPU Clusters and evaluating Databricks for an integrated workflow. It identifies NVIDIA DGX Cloud Lepton with a current NeMo stack as the strongest architectural fit, subject to validation.

docs/COMMERCIAL_LLM_PRETRAINING_PLATFORMS_RESEARCH.md

Training from new weights

The research defines training from scratch as initializing new model weights and training them on an admitted corpus. It distinguishes this process from continued pretraining, fine-tuning and reinforcement learning that retain an existing checkpoint's ancestry.

docs/COMMERCIAL_LLM_PRETRAINING_PLATFORMS_RESEARCH.md

Procurement checks

The comparison distinguishes managed training platforms, managed clusters and contracted training services by supplier scope and customer responsibilities. It proposes working pilots before substantial commitments and checks for capacity, complete costs, preview conditions and checkpoint export.
