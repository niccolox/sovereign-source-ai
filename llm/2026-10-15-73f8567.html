New draft proposes shared records for LLM rights and contributor awards

The new LLM Rights and Content Awards draft proposes shared records for source permissions, attribution, provenance and contributor benefits. The project remains at specification stage.

15 Oct 2026 · commit 73f8567

Permission and contribution records

The proposal connects contributions with authorization evidence, use receipts and accepted award agreements. It separates legal rights from contractual participation and credit.

docs/LLM_RIGHTS_AND_CONTENT_AWARDS_SPEC.md

Contributor benefits

The draft proposes contributor benefits under accepted agreements, with separate records for monetary awards and recognition. It states that calling compensation an award does not make accrued payments discretionary.

docs/LLM_RIGHTS_AND_CONTENT_AWARDS_SPEC.md

Conformance requirements

The proposal defines roles for rights issuers, training consumers, serving consumers and awards operators. Proposed conformance statements distinguish simulated settlement from actual payment support. The draft reports no demonstrated interoperable implementations.

docs/LLM_RIGHTS_AND_CONTENT_AWARDS_SPEC.md

Proposal status and documentation

The draft identifies itself as an independent proposal without W3C endorsement. The documentation index and project specification now reference it. The project specification states that the proposal does not replace accepted agreements.
