Training research expands to twelve commercial offerings

The research adds Modal and Baseten to a twelve-provider comparison for proposed training from scratch. It proposes different candidates for small experiments, enterprise training and supplier-assisted model development.

Candidates by training need

The research proposes Modal and AWS SageMaker AI for a first small independent model. It proposes AWS as the default enterprise bidder and Cerebras for a supplier-assisted model development proposal. These recommendations assess fit, not measured performance or lowest cost.

Modal and Baseten assessments

The research describes Modal’s small-model training example and Baseten’s custom training scripts. It proposes checks for checkpoint recovery, execution limits, regional costs and usable model delivery. Baseten’s suitability for a specific pretraining implementation remains an inference that needs a pilot.

Starting-weight checks

The research distinguishes training with newly initialized weights from further training of existing models. It identifies Tinker, Together Custom Training and Baseten Loops as products whose documented operations do not establish a from-scratch route. It proposes supplier demonstrations of launch configurations to verify starting weights.
