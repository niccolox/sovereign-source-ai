SkyRL research adds a proposed training workflow and evaluation criteria

The research document now explains a proposed SkyRL training loop using admitted documents, approved tools and reviewed scoring criteria. It separates learning scores from proposed creator payment eligibility.

Proposed training workflow

The proposed workflow lets a model attempt tasks, scores the outcomes and updates its parameters. Evaluation then uses tasks excluded from training. SkyRL coordinates attempts and updates; the operator supplies the model, task material and scoring rules.

Evidence and permission checks

A proposed scientific task checks supporting citations, required credit and source-use receipts against actual tool activity. Missing evidence should prompt abstention. Proposed tool-service permission checks would block unauthorized access independently of learned behavior.
