Proposed performance evaluation

The review separates Nemotron Super comparison results from the proposed Ultra target. It proposes measuring cost per verified outcome, including retries, tools and validation. Proposed acceptance criteria cover correctness, authorized source access, supported citations and valid tool behavior.
