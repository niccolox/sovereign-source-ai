Sovereign Source AI — Logo Concepts

Patriotic USA inspired · dark-site aesthetic · unique, not cliché

The Sovereign Star

Three horizontal bars (flag stripe echo) in red-white-blue, topped by a single five-point star in patriotic red with a blue outline. The star sits sovereign above the bars.

The Sovereign Shield

A minimalist shield (sovereignty / defense) in deep navy with a red border. A white five-point star sits at center — the star as the core, the shield as the guarantee. Clean, distinctive, not a flag.

The Convergent Star

Three vertical bars (in red, white-tinted, blue) converge upward into a single five-point star. The structure reads as both stripes and a star simultaneously — sovereign source emerging from three foundations.

The Star Mark

A single bold five-point star — red, with a navy inner star as a counterpoint. No bars, no shield. Just the star, treated as a monogram mark. The most minimal option, closest to a brand mark you'd use as a favicon.

The Sovereign Loop

A twisted three-stripe form — reads as stripes but with a Möbius-loop tension suggesting continuity, sovereignty, and a closed system. A white star sits at center. Suggests both the flag and the "no dependency without provenance" principle.

Star + Wordmark

The star mark (from concept 4) paired with a clean wordmark. "SovSrc" in white, "SOVRSRC.AI" in muted gray below. This is the full brand lockup — what you'd use in the site header and favicon.
